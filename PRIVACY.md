# Privacy policy

This policy covers the Webtoons Chapter Preloader userscript and the Toonlight Preloader browser extension ("Toonlight Preloader for WEBTOON"), which run the same code.

## In short

Toonlight Preloader collects no data. Nothing you do is recorded, nothing is stored, and nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## What is stored

Nothing. The preloader has no settings and keeps no storage of any kind: no extension storage, no cookies, no local storage.

## What is not collected

- No personal information, account details, passwords or payment information.
- No browsing history, reading history or page content.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry of any kind.

## Network requests

The extension makes no network requests of its own and loads no remote code. On a WEBTOON chapter page (`www.webtoons.com/*/viewer*` and the mobile site's `m.webtoons.com/*/viewer*`), it tells the page to load the chapter's own images right away instead of as you scroll. Those images come from WEBTOON's own image server, the same requests the page makes when you scroll to them. The only difference is timing: the chapter (on the mobile site, the part of it the page shows) is downloaded when you open it, even the panels you might not scroll to.

## Permissions

- **Access to WEBTOON chapter pages (www.webtoons.com and m.webtoons.com):** to start the chapter's image downloads early. The extension asks for no other permission and runs on no other page or site.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published here, in the project's repository. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/hervad/webtoons-chapter-preloader/issues.

Toonlight Preloader is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by NAVER WEBTOON.
